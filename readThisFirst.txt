This project was inspired by my love for Kobe Bryant and my interest in analyzing his basketball stats. 
I designed it for basketball fans like myself—especially Kobe’s die-hard supporters who enjoy digging into performance data. 
While building the visualization,I faced several challenges: sometimes clicking a season circle didn’t lead to the right page,
and my initial idea to represent scoring zones using a detailed half-court graphic turned out to be too complex.
I ultimately simplified it to a semi-circular scoring zone representing shot distance. 
These compromises helped keep the design functional and clear for the intended audience, 
while still capturing the essence of Kobe’s legacy.

What is my website:
a kobe scoring statistic
First page has data circles showing the total points in each of his seasons. 
When click on a circle, it will lead you to another page with a scoring zone visualization (by myself),
it shows how many shots he scored and attempted in each zones (Freethrows, 2 points shot, 3 points shot).

What is inside this folder.
The main javascript that you should start with is 'main.js'. The seasons folder is for 
the second page which have the scoring zone visualization in it.
